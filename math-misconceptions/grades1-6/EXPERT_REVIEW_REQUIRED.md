# Expert review required before use with pupils

> **NOTICE. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** The material in this repository about pupils in grades 1–6 (ages about 7–12) has not been reviewed by a data protection officer, a lawyer or a research ethics committee.
>
> Until the sign-off in `docs/privacy/expert_review_signoff_template.md` is completed by the named experts:
>
> - do not collect, log or store data about pupils in grades 1–6;
> - do not send pupil answers, work or messages to any third-party or AI service;
> - do not enable the per-pupil `Tally` in `tools/buggy_rules/`;
> - use synthetic data for development and testing.
>
> See `docs/privacy/README.md`.
>
> *Oppilastietoja ei kerätä eikä tallenneta ennen kuin tietosuojavastaava, juristi ja tutkimuseettinen toimikunta ovat arvioineet suunnitelman.*
