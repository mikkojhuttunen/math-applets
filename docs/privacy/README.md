# Privacy and ethics review pack: grades 1–6

> **EXPERT REVIEW REQUIRED. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** These documents were drafted by an AI assistant. They must be reviewed and decided by a data protection officer, legal counsel and a research ethics committee before any data about pupils in grades 1–6 is collected or any pupil input is sent to a third-party or AI service.

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `grades1-6_privacy_review_briefing.md` | Facts checked, questions for the experts, defaults proposed, gate before sign-off |
| `data_inventory_grades1-6.csv` | Every data item the design could process, with a default and blank columns for the experts' decisions |
| `expert_review_signoff_template.md` | Form the experts complete. Status is "not reviewed" until they do. |

Developers may build and test with synthetic data. Nothing here authorises a pilot.
